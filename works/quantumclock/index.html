quantum clock

A strange clock that explores how reliability emerges from randomness.

A handbuilt circuit causes electrons to quantum tunnel across a nanometers-thick barrier. These random quantum events cause the duration of each "second" to be different. But over minutes and hours, the clock converges to tell accurate time.1

There's a ~50% chance that the clock will be off by a second. But it is virtually impossible that it will be off by a minute——in fact, any of the following would be more likely:

  • Correctly choosing a single random grain of sand from all the beaches on Earth
  • Flipping a coin and getting heads 73 times in a row
  • Winning the powerball lottery 3 times back-to-back
